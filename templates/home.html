Agriculture is the heartbeat of our planet — it sustains life, nurtures communities, and feeds the world. Every year, farmers face crop loss due to unpredictable climate, soil nutrient imbalances, and unmanaged diseases. CropSpectra combines Deep Learning Vision and Precision Agronomy to provide Indian farmers with actionable, real-time intelligence.

Comprehensive Smart Farming Tools

  • Crop Recommendation Engine: Rule-based matching of 25+ Indian crops based on ICAR agronomic thresholds, state suitability, soil classification, and seasonal weather.
  • Smart Fertilizer Calculator: Farm-scale nutrient dosage calculations (DAP, Urea, MOP, SSP), multi-stage split application schedules, and pH amendment guidance.
  • Leaf Disease Vision: Deep Learning detection across Tomato, Potato, Pepper, Maize, Rice, and Wheat with out-of-distribution leaf verification.
  • Multilingual Farmer Advisory: Integrated audio playback and translation in Hindi (हिंदी), Bengali (বাংলা), Marathi (मराठी), and Bhojpuri (भोजपुरी).

How It Works

CropSpectra brings modern agricultural science directly to farmers in three simple steps:

1. Select Advisory Tool – Choose Crop Recommendation, Fertilizer Dosage, or Disease Vision.

2. Enter Field Data / Upload – Provide soil test values, weather data, or upload a crop leaf image.

3. Receive Instant Advisory – Get customized, actionable insights with audio explanations in regional languages.
